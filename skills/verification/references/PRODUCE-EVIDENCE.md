# Produce Verification Evidence

Collect the evidence required by accepted requirements and any approved verification plan while performing the work.
Follow the applicable technique procedure.
Record the command, procedure, environment, observation, and artifact needed to interpret each result.
Run every mandatory gate established by applicable authority before completion.

When an approved verification plan exists, the actor may strengthen its evidence or use a mechanically equivalent mechanism.
The actor must not weaken required confidence or change a materially required seam without amending the plan through its applicable authority.
If required evidence cannot be produced, stop the affected work and report the failed mechanism, observed result, and required decision or capability.
Do not treat missing evidence as success.

Evidence production is complete when every Verification Claim has interpretable evidence and every mandatory gate passes.
