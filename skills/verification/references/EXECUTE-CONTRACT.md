# Execute a Task Verification Contract

Collect the required evidence while implementing the behavior.
Follow the applicable technique procedure.
Record the command, procedure, environment, observation, and artifact needed to interpret each result.
Run every mandatory repository gate before completion.

The implementer may strengthen evidence or use a mechanically equivalent mechanism.
The implementer must not weaken required confidence or change an approved seam materially without amending the plan.
If required evidence cannot be produced, block implementation and report the failed mechanism, observed result, and required decision or capability.
Do not treat missing evidence as success.

Execution is complete when every Verification Claim has interpretable evidence and every mandatory gate passes.
