# Review Verification Evidence

Use this reference when the work explicitly requires review of a verification approach or its produced evidence.
Do not require a separate verification design artifact before review.

Anchor the review to the accepted result and the exact work being judged.
Ask whether the submitted evidence could distinguish a materially incorrect result from the accepted result.
Confirm that the evidence observes every boundary on which that judgment actually depends, corresponds to the relevant revision and environment, and is interpretable.
Do not reject sufficient evidence merely because another mechanism is more familiar or broader.
Do not demand a durable test when proportionate existing or one-time evidence is sufficient.

Report only material confidence gaps and explain what remains unsupported.
Distinguish an inability to perform the review from a completed review that finds the evidence insufficient.
The first is a tooling or capability limitation.
The second is a finding about the submitted evidence.
Do not express either case as success.
