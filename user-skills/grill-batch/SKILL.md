description: "[M] Batch the remaining grill questions into one response."
disable-model-invocation: true
---

Gather every remaining `Open point` from the grilling decision ledger into one list.

For each `Open point`, give the recommended answer and its evidence-based reason.

The batch is complete when every remaining `Open point` appears exactly once and has one recommended answer with an evidence-based reason.
